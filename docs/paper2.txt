Paper : DistilBERT, a distilled version of BERT: smaller,
faster, cheaper and lighter

Link :
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.01108

Main points :
1. Bert is the teacher and DistilBERT is the student.
2. Uses knowledge distillation during pre-training.
3. It reduces BERT's 12 transformer layer to 6 layers.
4. Student learns from both BERT's prediction and also internal representations.
5. It uses three losses : masked language modelling, distillation loss, cosine embedding loss.
6. It achieves about 97 per of the BERT's performance while being 40 per smaller and 60 per faster.
7. So it shows knowledge distillation can produce more compact general puprpose lanaguge models.


Summary :
Sanh et al. (2019) introduced DistilBERT, a compact version of 
BERT designed to reduce the computational and memory requirements 
of large pretrained language models while retaining most of their 
language understanding capabilities. Unlike earlier approaches that 
primarily applied knowledge distillation to task-specific models, 
DistilBERT performs distillation during the pre-training stage. 
The student model is trained using a combination of language 
modeling, knowledge distillation, and cosine-distance losses, 
enabling it to learn both linguistic representations and also the 
information encoded in the teacher model. DistilBERT reduces 
BERT’s size by approximately 40% and achieves around 97% of its 
language understanding performance while being approximately 60% 
faster, which demonstrates the effectiveness of knowledge distillation 
for creating efficient Transformer-based models.
